## Important Design Notes

The homepage should sell TapRank as one product first.

The four variants are:

1. Google Review
2. Instagram
3. Tripadvisor
4. Custom Logo + Branding

Google Reviews are TapRank's primary use case, but Instagram and Tripadvisor should remain clearly visible and should not feel secondary or hidden.

The website should be conversion-focused, visually clear and product-led.

Do not add unnecessary features, decorative clutter or invented product functionality.
